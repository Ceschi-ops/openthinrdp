When OpenThinRDP fits

Your desktops are Windows Remote Desktop

RD Connection Broker with session hosts, a Hyper-V VDI collection, or users who connect to their own Windows PC.

You want to keep old thin clients useful

Repurpose Dell, HP, Wyse or Fujitsu thin clients and small PCs with UEFI instead of paying a licence per device or replacing them.

You want simple management

A file share and a Windows tool instead of a management server: status, central settings, logo and signed updates in plain files.

You want RDP over UDP

The FreeRDP UDP transport makes sessions smoother on busy servers and lossy networks, with fallback to TCP.

When another system is better

If you need Citrix or VMware/Omnissa Horizon, ARM devices, vendor support or certified hardware, the commercial systems (IGEL OS, Dell ThinOS, Stratodesk NoTouch) are the right choice. If you want many protocols and are happy to build your own image, ThinStation is a long-standing open source option; openthinclient suits networks that prefer network boot with a management server.
