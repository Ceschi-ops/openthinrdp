# Code of conduct

OpenThinRDP follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient with people of every background and level of experience, discuss ideas and
code rather than people, accept that others may decide differently, and help newcomers find their way.
Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not
accepted, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and contributions that do not follow these rules, and may
block people who repeatedly or seriously break them.

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through a
[private report](https://github.com/OpenThinRdp/openthinrdp/security/advisories/new) on this repository (write
"Code of conduct" in the title). Every report is read and handled confidentially.
